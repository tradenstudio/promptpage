Questions

Does PromptPage read or store my data?

No. Pages are converted inside your browser. Nothing is sent to us — we have no servers. The only network request is validating your Pro license key with our payment provider.

How do I activate Pro?

After checkout you receive a license key by email. Open PromptPage's settings, paste the key, click Activate. Done.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes, one license works on up to 3 browsers. You can move it from the settings page at any time.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera and other Chromium browsers (version 116 or newer).
