PromptPage

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary

PromptPage does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. Page content is processed only inside your browser.

What the extension processes

When you click PromptPage, it reads the content of the current tab (or the tabs you choose to combine) to convert it into Markdown. This happens locally on your device. The result goes only to your clipboard, a file you download, or the AI website you choose to open.

What is stored

Your settings (selected template, AI destination, custom templates, token limit) are stored with Chrome's storage API, which may sync across your own browsers if you have Chrome sync enabled. Your Pro license key is stored locally on your device.

Network requests

The only request PromptPage makes to an outside service is sending your license key to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy (lemonsqueezy.com), to activate and periodically validate Pro. No page content, browsing history or personal information is included.

When you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy processes your payment as merchant of record under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details from them to deliver your license.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — read the page you click PromptPage on.
  • storage — save your settings.
  • contextMenus — add right-click actions.
  • All sites (optional) — requested only when you use "combine tabs", so the extension can read those tabs.

Analytics and tracking

None. PromptPage contains no analytics, ads or third-party trackers.
